Order blocks (green demand / red supply) and fair value gaps (blue / violet) are the live, unspent ones the EA's SMC engine reports. BOS (solid) and CHoCH (dashed) run from the swing to the candle that closed beyond it. ● marks the exact wick that swept a block, with its stop dashed; ✕ the candle that broke one. Unlock the chart (padlock) to zoom with the wheel and drag to look back; locked, scrolling just moves the page. Zones beyond the scale show as ▲ / ▼ tags on the edge.
