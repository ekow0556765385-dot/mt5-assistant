Honest limits

Readings can be up to about 15 seconds behind price: the EA sends every 5 seconds, the file bridge checks every 3, and Arbiter re-reads every 5. A decision that must happen inside a second belongs in the EA, not here. Blackwood does not place trades or tell you to take one.
