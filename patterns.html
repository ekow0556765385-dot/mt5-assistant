What the evidence says
Each line is an observation with a weight, not a forecast. Negative weights argue the trend survives; positive ones argue it is turning. The score is their sum, then reduced when Risk Radar has the pair flagged — a flagged window makes the evidence itself less trustworthy.

Which patterns actually worked last 30 days · this account
Every pattern at 70%+ confidence is recorded when it forms (within the last 2 closed bars), then priced again one and four hours later. "Worked" means price moved at least 8 pips in the pattern's direction — checked after 1 hour for M15/H1 patterns, after 4 hours for H4 and D1 patterns. Saved to your account, so your phone and desktop show the same record — it is your record, not a published statistic.

Support & resistance
Probability to hold = how often price was turned away from this level when it came back (softened so 2 of 2 reads 75%, not 100%). A medium further from price never scores below the one in front of it, because price has to get through the nearer one first. It describes what the level has done, not what it will do.
