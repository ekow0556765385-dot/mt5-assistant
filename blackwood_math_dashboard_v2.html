⚙ Dashboard Settings — All 6 Tools

01 — Expectancy

Spread Cost (R) is your universal cost-per-trade — set it once to cover the average spread + commission across whichever prop firm or broker you trade with. It's used in Expectancy (01) and Loss Asymmetry (05).

02 — Breakeven Win Rate

Your Reward:Risk setting stays exactly as you set it — it will not reset to 2.5 on live sync, reload, or new trades.

03 — Risk of Ruin

This is your own risk-per-trade assumption for the Risk of Ruin model — independent from the sync bar's global Risk % if you want to stress-test a different number.

04 — Gambler's Fallacy

No manual settings — this tool reads your live win rate and trade streak directly from closed trade history. Adjust win rate assumptions via Tool 02's Target Win Rate when you have no trade history yet.

05 — Loss Asymmetry

Uses your Risk/Trade % and Spread Cost from Tools 01 & 03/06 above — no separate settings needed. Adjust those and this table updates automatically.

06 — Position Sizing

Risk %, SL Pips, and Pair Type are saved to this browser and stay exactly as you set them — live balance updates only refresh the Balance field, never these.
