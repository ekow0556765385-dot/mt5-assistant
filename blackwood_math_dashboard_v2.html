Settings

01 — Expectancy

Automatic reads your average win and loss straight from your closed trades and keeps them current as you trade. Manual leaves the three fields below to you — useful for testing a system you have not traded yet, or one whose history is not in this account.

Your average win and loss in R, and what a round trip costs you in spread and commission. Leave costs at zero only if you genuinely trade for free.

02 — Breakeven Win Rate

The reward:risk you actually trade, and the win rate you are aiming to hold.

03 — Risk of Ruin

How much you risk per trade, and the drawdown you would consider unrecoverable.

06 — Position Sizing

Defaults for the sizer. Pip value is per standard lot in your account currency; the currency of your trading account, so nothing here needs converting by hand.

Dynamic keeps your risk at the same percentage of the balance, so the lot size rises as the account grows and falls as it draws down. Fixed keeps the lot size and stop exactly where you set them, so the money at risk never changes and the percentage moves instead. Choose one deliberately — they behave very differently through a losing run.

R Multiple

R is a trade's result measured in units of what it risked, so it needs the stop that was actually set. Where the broker recorded a stop, that stop is used. Where it did not — a trade closed manually with no stop attached, for instance — the fallback below is used instead, and those trades are marked in the history so you can see which figures rest on it. Set it to zero to exclude them from every calculation rather than estimate them.

07 — Stop Radar

How close a trade must get to its stop before it is called out, and how far ahead the warning banner looks.
